When a party member is downed or wounded with a roll above 15 within your view enter a state of panicked frenzy in a effort to save your party Members. At the end of each of your turn in state loss 5 mana in addition to any costs added by effects gained in this state. You can exit this state at the end of combat or when all parameters have been stabilized. This state can induce Mana Burn.

## Effects in Frenzy State
### Base - 5 Mana a turn
While in this state you are unable to be downed or suffer the effects of any received wounds. You receive a temporary +1 to Strength, and a 1d6 bonus to hit.
