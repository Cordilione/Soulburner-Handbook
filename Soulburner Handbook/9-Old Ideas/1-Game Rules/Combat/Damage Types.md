

## Piercing 

### Wounding Table

1-5 Large Bleed
	At the start of each their turn those afflicted take 1d4 damage. After 4 rounds have passed if this wound has not been treated the afflicted make a Fortitude save of 13, on a fail Raise the level of the bleed.

6-12 Deep Wound
	Afflicted Lose 5ft of Move and each time they perform a move action for every 5ft moved they take 1d4 damage.

13-18 Damaged Organ
	When the target attempts any action during their turn they must pass a dc 16 Fortitude save. On a fail the target losses 1 AP that turn 2 on a critical fail. If the target longer has enough AP for their attempted action after the AP lost they may perform a new action.

19-20 Pierced Stomach
	The target is afflicted with a 1d6 Bleed and on the end of any Move or Strike Action they take 1d8 Acid Damage.
