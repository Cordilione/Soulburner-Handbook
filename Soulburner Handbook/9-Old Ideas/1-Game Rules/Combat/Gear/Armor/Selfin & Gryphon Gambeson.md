An Arming Coat made from Selfin and padded with Gryphon Feathers. 

Selfin is known for its exceptional strength for a fiber however it comes at the cost of its flammability. This armor offers 2 ac instead of a Gambesons 1 but any fire damage reduces the armors Quality by 1. 

Gryphon Feathers natural form help Gryphons fly despite their massive size. The Fathers them self work a focuses for lighting spells but a large number is needed for any effect. This armor lightens ones step when mana is pushed through it. At a cost of 2 mana the wearer can move 5 additional feet a turn (this is a free action).
