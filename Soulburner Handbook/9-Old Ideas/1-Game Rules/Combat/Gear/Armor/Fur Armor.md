Unlike its more substantial hide counter part Fur armors have been thinned and softened. This softening is done to the point that for many in colder regions this is a common clothing. Weather made from a single large hide or Patched together from a smaller sections this Armors fur interior helps to trap body heat and keep of the cold. While in this armor you have 1 resistance to Cold but a 1 amplification (weakness) to Fire.
