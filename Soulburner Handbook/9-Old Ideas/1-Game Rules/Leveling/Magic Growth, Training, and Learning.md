
Magic along with much of your character growths as they goes the story your group will face. Weather you are a Scholarly mage hunting for Abjuration limits or the farm boy Despite the same his livestock or even his self. Magic will find its way into your life in this world. The only question is how your character chooses to face and utilize these aspects of the world. 

## Magic on Character Creation
---
When you prepare you character for the start of the story your DM is going to be giving you a number of character trainings your character may have. These trainings can be spent on you characters magical ability in addition to other aspects of yourself. Magic training at the start of a character is spent buying access to spells and Spell bases. We will explore this system using a noble pyromancer.
### Spell Trainings
---
The most straight forward way to gain magic is to buy into spells directly. This offers your character the ability to cast the spell they have been trained in however they lack the ability to use the 'parts' of that spell in other castings or work the spell at all.

As a pyromancer we may choose training in the spell Ignition. Once however we will also have to pick a 'level' of the spell we are trained in. As a pyromancer we will likely want to get a stronger or more mana efficient version of the spell meaning it will likely cost us more than a single character training. 
##### Pyromancers Example
Our pyromancer will buy into only 1 spell as they will instead focus their points into Spell bases. However they have chosen to take a higher level of Ignition to make up for their lack of flexibility with the spell. 
**Expert Ignition**
	 AP: 2
	 Mana Cost: 10
	 Range: 30ft
	 Target: 1
	 Damage: 2d4 Fire, 2d6 if target is in Melee range

As this is a higher level of the spell this costs our Pyromancer 2 trainings.


### Spell Base Trainings
---
Bases are a more complex and expensive system to buy into for characters. This is because they represent not a single spell but a understanding of underlying magical ideas. Bases are a group of properties and systems that a caster can mix to cast finished spells. When learning a base it often starts from a single spell that the caster has become extremely failure (familiar) with. It is from here they learn more properties they can use to cast new variations or even new entire spells from the same base.

This system is where most dedicated mages focus their effort as it offers true control over ones magic as such our Pyromancer will be spending more of their trainings here then they would on stand alone spells.
##### Pyromancers Example
Our Pyromancer will only be buying into 1 base along with their previous spell. When buying a base characters need to pick their 'hallmark' or first spell of the base. For our Pyromancer this will be Flaming Ray. Unlike spells when we learn this spell it it always at its base level.

**Flaming Ray**
	AP: 2
	Mana Cost: 15
	Duration: 3 per turn, ends on new castings of the spell. 
	Range: 60ft
	Target: 1
	Damage: 1d6 on hit, 1d6 for each turn the target end in the same location.

This spell example if from the base of Lingering Flame spells as such this is now where we are trained. This Training has cost us only a single training point at this point however since it is a Spell base our Pyromancer will buy a few properties to be able to modify this spell with.

Currently this spell targets only 1 tile however lingering flame spells can be used to cover far greater spaces. Our pyromancer will spend another training on buying the property to target up to 5 connected tiles or 20ft. However this can not be done for free with this same spell. When our pyromancer does this increased targeting every extra tile will increase the base cost of the spell by 3 and the duration cost by 1. The properties of bases and their effect on spells  will be outlined in the base if it from the text however it is always encouraged to play with bases and properties that are made between player and DM so feel free to ask and try crazy things.

### Pyromancer Overview
---
Our pyromancer will spend the rest of his trainings in other places. Despite this however he still has a very strong magical start for his stories beginning. He has invested a total of 4 of his Character trainings and has access to a reliable single target damaging spell and a magical base that can grow along side him remaining endlessly useful. 



## Magic Leveling
---
While it is good to know magic at the start if your adventure being able to learn new spells expand bases or even unlock a unseen magical capability is important. Characters can grow and train all the same thing as in character creation however all of this must now be done in the story. Downtime and story moments become your tool for not only character expression but growth as well. As we give examples any describe the systems of character growth in magic we will keep using the pyromancer we started before. 

### Dice and Moments
---
Just as all ways your character will grow during the story your magic growth as system is a number of events that allow to gain understanding that can be used to improve and learn. Magic Understanding rolls are always done as ***1d20+Soul+DM/Story Mod***. Soul may be swapped out at times Arcane when the learning moment is more structed in form like sigils or Education form magical schools. Magic has a number of moments that can allow you to make these checks. We will Explore these in order of how much understanding they will offer your character. 

##### Magical Observation
These are story moments of watching and experiencing magic in a way that offers you insight into how it works. These moments are more than just having someone cast a spell in front of you. They need to offer a story beat and happen in moments where it wont break gameplay to perform the roll. 

A bad example of a Observation moment would be our pyromancer wanting to do one of these checks in combat because an opposing mage used a flame whip spell. While this is a moment the pyromancer would be watching unless the DM allows it there wouldn't be the time to try and gain understanding from the casting. The pyromancer is in a fight no matter how much they watch the spell their mind must be focused on the fight. Plus doing a understating check after every enemy casting would be a hassle for the flow of the game at the table. 

An example of a a good observation moment is our pyromancer spending a night watch in camp watching the flame and how it dances. This a moment that can allow for our pyromancer to understand more about the flame he wields. Its not a moment of raw magic however it serves as a great example for them to understand fire in a more natural way. This type of moment is not one the player could be reliant on however. It cant be done every watch shift for both table play as having to roll a check every time the player takes watch slows down to the game. But also because only so much could be learned this way so DM should not allow this moment to used constantly by a player. 

These examples hopefully serve to give you a good feeling for when players and DM should allow and make Observation learning checks. Due to the possible conflicts this form of learning can offer due to its story based nature the game can be played completely without this form.

##### Magic Training
Training just like for all other areas of a character is the most common way a character will grow. Training can be handled in a range of ways however for now we will explain the most basic and expansion for game "realism".

The basic of training is during the down time of a story your character may work on themselves instead of using this time as a rest. For every hour spent training with you will be allowed to make a understanding check. Understanding checks from training will offer a constant 2 understanding on a success and 4 on a critical. If your down time is less than an hour you may still spend the time training however you will need to reach a higher DC in order to pass. This increased DC along with the training DC are full in DM hands however for groups that want a hard system to fallow they can use the fallowing system. ***8 + Soul + held understanding*** This is not a end all be all system, story and DM feeling is encouraged over this system however when unsure it should be good enough. 

An optional additional system that can be used Session training. This is an  additional chance to make training checks that is done at the end of a game session. It is simply the DM offering additional rolled based on the actions performed in the session. An example could be if out pyromancer pushed himself into mana burn maintaining a Fire wall needed for a combat the DM may offer them to use that as a training moment at the end of the session. As this is another system that lacks a rigid form it is entirely option however it is encouraged as it allows for growth from actions instead of only down time.
