A white metal that shine similarly to stainless steal. when tarnished it is easy to mistake as poorly cared for iron this metal has little use in most metallurgy however a number of gear and pendants have been made of the metal for its use in magic. The metals natural structure works like a amplifier for mental magics. This leads to the longer term use of effects having a lesser effect on those who utilize the metal as a arcane sigil passing spells and mana through it in addition to the needs of their magic. 

The effect of the meatal scales just all foci do with size and proximity.
