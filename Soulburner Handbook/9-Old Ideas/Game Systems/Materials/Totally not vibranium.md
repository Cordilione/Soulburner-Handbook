a strange metal with the ability to store absorbed kinetic energy like a battery and release it as a wave of force at a later date.

when used to create armor gain resistance 5 to bludgeoning track all negated bludgeoning damage to a max of 25. at 25 create a shockwave effecting all squares adjacent to you dealing 2d6 bludgeoning damage to all targets in range excluding you and reset tracker to 0.

when used to create a weapon every time damage is rolled add half the physical damage as bludgeoning to the final total.

another option is to require some amount of mana to trigger the effect consistently as an example spend 15 mana on a successful melee attack turning it into a discount smite of sorts.

the metal would have to cost an arm and a leg cause the only real way to shape it would be a skilled transmutation mage or by mold casting as traditional smithing would be quite dangerous if not impossible.
