# Cooking Materials Explained
---
### Cooking Sets
---
**Rough Cooking Set**
	A simple set of tools and open fire is all you have. The adventures pot is often enough to make great meals for those living on the rough side of life.
**Basic Cooking Set**
	A kitchen and its niceties. Ranging from the inns tools to your grandma best a basic cooking set can perform most cooking styles.
**Professional Cooking Set**
	The grandest of kitchens everything from grand ovens to custom machines for sugar plums and turducken.
