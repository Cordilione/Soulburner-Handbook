# Rouge Flair points
Your flair points can be sent to perform a number of actions across your class and RP. 

### Flair for RP
Spending your flair can be to perform a strike that makes sense in the moment. this is not restricted to strikes in the rule set. If we have failed to make a ima bite this mans hand then work as a table to make that bite strike your selves. The cost in flair is decided by your DM so be ready for what ever number they give.

### Roguish Actions
Your class gives you a number of rouge actions that will need this flair to perform. These will vary across combat and down town but all represent the unkind and uncaring actions you may wish to perform.

## Flair Regen
You get back flair points after a long rest and can recover more during the day by "plotting". this is spending time to mentaly map what your going to do this time does not count as a rest but must be done during rest time. For every 30 mins plotting you regain 1 flair.
