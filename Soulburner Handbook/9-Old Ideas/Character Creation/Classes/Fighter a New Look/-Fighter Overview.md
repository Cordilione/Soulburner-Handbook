
### Ability Score: Strength

### Class Stats
HP: 10
Fortitude: 4
Willpower: 2
Reflex: 3
### Class Skills
Spend 3 Points across the Following Skills
- Acrobatics
- Athletics
### Class Trainings
Choose 1 Melee Weapon Style and 1 Armor Style then become Trained in one an Expert in the Other.

### Class Action
**Execute** for **3 AP** select a target that is incapacitated *(will become keep word but covers ideas like prone asleep bound and the like)* and make a challenge roll against them. You roll an attack with your active weapon (include all mods) and the target rolls a CON check (All mods included). On your success the target is dropped to 0 hp and fails 1 death check. On a targets success you continue as you were making a basic strike against the target.
