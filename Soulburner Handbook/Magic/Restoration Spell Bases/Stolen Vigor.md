# Core Effect
---

A Complex spell base that is difficult to master and whos effects can only target ones self. 

**Requirements**: Constitution and Soul of at least 3 each

You burn Mana in place in physical resources like energy or adrenaline to fuel your body. This doesn't come free as it cost not only mana but the return of the aliments at a later time if not performed properly. The versatility of this base leaves it inefficient in mana use compared to more focused bases and as a struggle for many casters to learn and adopt.

### Heal a Wound
---
**Temporarily**: 3 * Wound Level (placement on wound table)
**Time Scale**: 2 * Number of Rounds
**End of Time Effects**
	0 Mana - Track all damage the would be taken and receive it at the end of                the spells effects.
	3 Mana - The wound opens in a Explosive manor causing you to take its level             d4's of damage.
	5 Mana - You suffer no ill effect at the end of the of the healing period.

**Permanently**: 12 * Wound Level

# Examples
---

**Stabilize Major Wound** 
	Cost 25 mana
	For the next 5 rounds ignore all effects of a Major wound you have received, at the end of the 5 rounds take 4d4 Physical damage.

**Mana Fueled Strength**
	Cost 10
	Scaling upkeep of 3
	Gain a Temp +1 to STR and at the start of each of your turn if you wish to keep the effect pay a upkeep of 10 plus 3 for each round effect has been active. Can not be cast again until effect has not been active for at least 2 rounds
