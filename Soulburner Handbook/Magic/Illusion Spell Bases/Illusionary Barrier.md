# Core Effect
---
You create a purely visual barrier occupying a space entirely within your view. 
# Cost Scaling
---
### Barrier Generation
---
**Base cost**: 10
Base size is step below casters size with a max area of 5 grids

**Increased Area**: 2 per Grid
**Increased Hight**: 2 per 2 feet

### Barrier Duration
---
**Upkeep Cost**: Cost of spell cast
**Duration Cost** 5 per Round

### Discovery DC
---
**Base Discovery DC** 13

**Increase DC for Visual Identification** 3 per +1 to DC

# Examples
---

**Illusionary Flame Wall**
	**Cost**: 16
	**Upkeep**: 16
	**Size**: Small and max Area of 5 squares (25ft)
	**Effect**: You Summon an illusionary wall of flame that occupying a max of 25ft of visual space. To be identified visually as fake target must beat a DC 15 Perception(Wis) Check.
