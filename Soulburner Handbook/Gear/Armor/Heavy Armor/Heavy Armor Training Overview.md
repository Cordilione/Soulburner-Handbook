# Untrained
---
## Penalty 
When untrained within the heavy armor but still Wearing their great protection, one has many disadvantages. You can not cast spells that require movement, your strides lose 10ft of movement

**Camel Back:** you ignore 5ft of the movement penalty of heavy armor.

**Directed Blow:** When trained in Heavy armor one starts to utilize its full protective capabilities, you can better direct hits toward the stronghold of your armor as a reaction gain toughness equal to your armor bonus (example 4 for half plate) until end of turn, after this effect resolves you cannot use again till your next turn.
