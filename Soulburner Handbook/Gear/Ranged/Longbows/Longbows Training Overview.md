# Untrained
---
## Penalty 
**Arrows:** Without training most underestimate the ability of a good arrow because of this you are **unable to add arrow damage to attacks** or use magical arrows in combat.

**Hitting your mark:** Shooting bows in no easy feat from the difficulty in holding and drawing a bow to knowing how an arrow will fly. Because of this untrained shooters take a **-2 to hit**.

# Trained
---
## Ability
**Piercing blow:** In combat it is rare that a long-bowmen can fire with their full strength when they can however these shots are even more dangerous. This type of shot takes **2 Actions** to perform however it will always **ignore 2 AC**.(This number can be modified by materials)

# Expert
---
## Ability
**Field Shot:** With further training Long-bowmen learn to help their shots go farther while maintain their accuracy. This allows them to **extend all longbow ranges by 20 ft**.

**Firing Position:** Longbow of old fought in formation and numbers against their enemy. While this form of combat is far rarer today the advantage of a station position remains. **If you have not moved in the last or current turn your Reloads become a free action.**
