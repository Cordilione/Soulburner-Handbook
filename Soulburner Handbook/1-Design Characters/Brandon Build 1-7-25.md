# Lore / Idea backdrop
---
A man who knows the pain of war wished to lessen it through healing the injured and comforting those who's will has been broken.
## Start of Adventuring
---
When his lord called for men to fight he answered the call. There he made many friends and at first was hopeful that the conflict he was in would be a short one. But war is no place for hope, what fallowed was months of lengthy marches and brutal battles the saw his friends that he made along the way fall one by one. When the war was over he found no respite at his home. There he learned that his family's farm was burned down and parents along with it. He wandered the planes aimlessly hoping to find somewhere to build a new home, but the faces of his lost friends and the screams of those who died in battle fallowed him were ever he went. Then one day he wandered drunk into a temple of a God he did not know. There the people of the temple washed him up and gave him a job. Slowly this job became less of a way to survive and more of recompence to the temple for not only saving him from the streets, but himself and later he became a member of there number. Now with war set to engulf the Plains he has a new drive to lessen the pain of those who will fight it and has set off to be a healer.
