# Lore / Idea backdrop
---
A no name pickpocket fighting for his friends and family.
## Start of Adventuring
---
Grew up on the streets of freisburg parents died young had to steal to survive made friends with whores and street rats heard about bandits getting uppity starting to expand. Not wanting that to get anywhere near his home he and a few others joined the levy to fight on the frontline to make sure the bandits don't take one step more. Not a stranger to a fight but this will be the first time running isn't an option.
