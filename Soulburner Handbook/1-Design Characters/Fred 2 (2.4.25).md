# Abilities
**Elven sign Full Moon:** Once per combat you can roll insight vs one targets deception to learn if one stat of your choosing is higher or lower then your own score 

**Blade Catch:** If a melee attack weapon misses you, you can use your reaction to catch the blade. The target can then choose to spend a reaction to make a contested Athletics check to get there weapon back, on a fail you continue to hold the blade and the target can not make more attacks with that weapon. The target can spend an action to automatically break free of the hold  

**Flyby:** If a ranged weapon attack misses you, as a reaction you can throw the projectile back make a ranged attack throw (1d20+dex) at disadvantage to one target you can see on hit it dose your Dex in damage and any effects the projectile had activates

**Defensive Roll:** As a reaction you may use a reflex check in place of AC (unsure)
