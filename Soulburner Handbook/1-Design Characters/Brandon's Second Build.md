# Lore / Idea backdrop
---
A strong fighter that uses traps, magical and alchemical materials and planning to take down strong foes.
## Start of Adventuring
---
A Gnomish adventurer who specializes in the hunting and killing of monsters. With his small stature his has learned to deal with the beasts by researching them and using the proper tools to bring them down, be it with trap, poison, foci, or magic spell. He has joined the fight thinking that his skills can be of use in the conflict.
