# Goal outline for Environment

Show not war but its results on both land and the lives of man. The actions of the F.O.P may have saved a land live Fiving but it comes at a cost. Even in a war of only a few solders the lives of all in the region will be touched from the poor and struggling to rich and mighty just as the land its forms around war so will they.  

Player must face the cost of the war they now wage even if new recruits and untrained hands. testing the characters resolve and willingness to fight this war. Does the reality push them to greater furver or do they question their new home nation and its right to live.
